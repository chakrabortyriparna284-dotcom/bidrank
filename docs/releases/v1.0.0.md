# BidRank 1.0.0

_Released September 19, 2026_

BidRank is a full stack pay to rank SaaS leaderboard where founders compete for top ranking positions through transparent dynamic bidding, gaining verified visibility and high intent buyer traffic.

## Highlights

* **Public Leaderboard and Outbid Experience**: View live rankings across all time, weekly, and daily rolling windows. Click to outbid any competitor and claim their position instantly.
* **Founder Dashboard**: Track all your listed SaaS products in one place with live statistics on clicks received, total bids invested, and your best rank.
* **Public SaaS Profiles**: Share your dedicated product page featuring your current rank, outbound link, and complete historical bid progression timeline.
* **Stripe Payment Engine**: Secure checkout sessions handle initial listing deposits and incremental outbid payments with automatic ranking fulfillment.
* **Admin Control Center and Seed Engine**: Platform operators can inspect platform revenue, suspend suspicious listings, and seed fifteen realistic SaaS products with one click.

## Improvements

* Dynamic price delta calculation automatically tells you the exact dollar amount needed to outbid any listing or claim the top rank.
* Rolling timeframe counters reset automatically through scheduled background tasks, keeping the daily and weekly competition fresh.
* Outbound click tracking records every visitor navigating to product websites.
* Clean dark mode visual design with gold, silver, and bronze podium cards for top three positions.

## Automated Testing

* Complete test coverage across 8 test suites and 37 automated tests verifying bidding constraints, timeframe ranking, checkout fulfillment, dashboard aggregation, and profile history.
